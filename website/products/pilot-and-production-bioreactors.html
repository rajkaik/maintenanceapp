Why our bioreactors stand out

  • Fully automated with BioPhantom©: complete automation for precise control and reproducible results.
  • Flexible and powerful: bottom-mounted magnetic stirrer ensures perfect mixing for cells or microbes at every scale.
  • Fully integrated stainless-steel system: thermo-control, sterile filters and load cells built in for safe, reliable operation.
  • Precision process control: fine-tune stirrer speed, level, temperature, DO, pH, redox, pressure, gas flow and feed pumps with ease.
  • Expandable and compatible: easily integrate 3rd-party instruments, sensors and equipment. Optional gas mixing station, gas analyzer or turbidity sensor for extra monitoring power.

Proven in real-world applications

From vaccine production to probiotic starter cultures, yeast fermentation and mammalian cell cultivation, our systems deliver consistent, scalable results.

Made for small and mid-sized companies

Get all the reliability of commercial-scale stainless-steel bioreactors without the complexity and cost of large industrial systems. Ideal for R&D, pilot runs and early-stage production.
